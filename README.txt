5. Do not claim guaranteed returns, fabricated member counts, fake scarcity, or guaranteed winning trades.
6. Pixel tracking cannot guarantee real human traffic or prevent every bot/click-fraud event. Use Meta invalid-traffic reporting and campaign quality checks.

TRACKING
